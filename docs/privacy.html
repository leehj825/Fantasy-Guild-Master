What the game stores

Your guild's progress (buildings, heroes, settings) is saved on your own device (browser or app storage). We do not run a server for the game, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address or account details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows a banner ad and optional rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. Ads are served by Google. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are used, and you can change your choice later in the app's privacy options where offered.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob & AdSense data usage. You can reset or opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded ads are always optional: you only see one if you tap a button offering a bonus, and the game can be played fully without watching any.
