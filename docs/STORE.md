# Play Store release checklist

Everything here is a draft for you to review before publishing.

## 1. Accounts and ids
- AdMob app id and units are already in the app (release uses the real ids, debug uses Google test ids).
- Play Console: create the one-time in-app product **`starter_pack`** (price e.g. $1.99).
- Repository secrets for signed release builds: `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_BASE64`, `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`, `ANDROID_KEY_ALIAS`, `ANDROID_KEY_PASSWORD` (workflow: `.github/workflows/android-release.yml`).
- Privacy policy: publish `docs/privacy.html` (GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → deploy from `main` `/docs`), fill in the e-mail and date, and paste the URL into Play Console and AdMob.

## 2. Store listing (English)
**Title:** Fantasy Guild Master  
**Short description (80):** Build a low-poly fantasy guild, lead heroes on live 3D expeditions, and grow a living town.  
**Full description:**  
Run your own adventurers' guild. Build a tavern, forge and market, recruit heroes with their own personalities, and send them on expeditions you can watch in 3D. Tap to dodge boss attacks, cast skills, and decide when to retreat.

- Heroes with traits, bonds, rivalries, personal stories and families
- A town that grows: farms, bakery, roads, wells, fountains and decorations
- Live expeditions with bosses, weaknesses, relics and the Endless Dive
- Daily rewards, a free monthly pass, daily and challenge dives
- Seasons, festivals, disasters, trade caravans, pets and collections
- Plays offline. English, Korean and partial Japanese.

## 4. Graphics
Screenshots are generated by `node tools/store_screenshots.cjs` into `docs/store/` (phone portrait 1080×1920 and landscape 1920×1080). Still needed from you: a 512×512 app icon (the launcher icon in `android/app/src/main/res` can be exported) and a 1024×500 feature graphic.

## 5. Data safety form (answers that match the app)
- Data collected: **Device or other IDs** (advertising ID, by AdMob) and **App activity / diagnostics** collected by AdMob; purpose: advertising. Not sold. Shared with Google (ads).
- No account, no personal info collected by the developer. All game data stays on the device.
- Purchases: processed by Google Play.
- Data deletion: uninstalling removes all local data.
- Content rating questionnaire: fantasy violence (cartoon), no gambling, no user-generated content, ads shown.
- Target audience: 13+ (matches the privacy policy text). Choose "ads" = yes, "in-app purchases" = yes.

## 6. Before you submit
1. Run the **Android release** workflow and download the signed AAB/APK from the release.
2. Test on a real device: purchase flow with a licensed tester account, banner and rewarded ads (never tap your own live ads), notifications permission prompt (Android 13+).
3. Check the CI is green (the free-minutes limit on the GitHub account blocked the last runs; fix billing first).
